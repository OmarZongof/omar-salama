01 — About

Turning ambiguous problems
into shipped products.

I joined SirApp Technology in June 2025 as its first engineer. There was an owner, a manager, and two Android apps being built by an outside company — and nobody who could tell whether they would work.

I started as the bridge: testing every flow, documenting what was broken, and turning business needs into specs. When the handed-over code proved unfit for production, I led the decision to rebuild — two Flutter apps on Supabase, admin and wallet dashboards in .NET 8 + React, a company portal, and a payment system that sends withdrawals to NBE's CorPay network (run by EBC) over a secure VPN.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from AASTMT, where I led Sortify, an AI waste-sorting system. I still think like a designer — that's where the UI/UX side of what I build comes from.
